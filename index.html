customer retention

Finding where churn concentrates

I compared churn across 7,043 sample customers and checked how tenure affects the contract comparison.

What I found Month-to-month customers show 42.7% churn; two-year customers, 2.8%. This is an association, not a causal effect.

Suggested action Pilot improved onboarding for newer month-to-month customers.

How I’d measure it Measure incremental retention against a randomised control group, net of offer costs.
