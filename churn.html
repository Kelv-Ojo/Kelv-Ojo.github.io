Finding where churn concentrates

Which customer groups merit deeper retention research?

Area
Customer retention
Tools
SQL · Python · pandas
Scope
Historical / educational analysis

The finding

1,869 of 7,043 customers are labelled churned (26.5%). Month-to-month customers show 42.7% churn, versus 2.8% for two-year contracts. The month-to-month, 0–12 month segment shows 51.4% across 1,994 customers.

How I approached it

Cleaned the IBM Telco educational sample, preserved 11 missing TotalCharges values, checked unique customer IDs, and reconciled SQL segment counts with pandas. Charts include illustrative Wilson intervals.

What I would do next

Investigate onboarding and service friction for newer month-to-month customers. Test a randomized pilot and measure incremental retention net of offer cost.

Scope and limitations

Cross-sectional sample proportions are not monthly churn hazards. Contract length is associated with churn; this analysis cannot establish that longer contracts cause retention. No commercial savings are claimed.

Recommendation and success measures

Proposed action — Pilot improved onboarding for newer month-to-month customers.

How I’d measure success — Measure incremental retention against a randomised control group, net of offer costs.
