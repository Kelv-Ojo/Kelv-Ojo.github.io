Which cereal products deserve pricing attention?

Oliver Wyman Data & Analytics Analyst Simulation, October 2026. This is an educational exercise, not employment or a live client engagement.

Business question

Help a supermarket identify important products consistently, using loyalty-card sales measures and existing importance flags.

Check the data before modelling

  • 811 raw product-month records; 46 customer counts missing (5.67%).
  • October absent throughout the raw extract. Sixteen zero-sales rows retained for clarification.
  • Whitespace standardisation reduces 78 product names to 76. A probable spelling variant needs confirmation.
  • No exact duplicates or conflicting importance flags. Partial-year products require care in comparisons.

Model and validation

The separately supplied complete file has 885 rows and 75 products. I used product-level mean sales, customer count, price per kilogram, wallet share and stock cost. Whole products were split into 60 training and 15 test products to avoid leakage between monthly records.

Five-fold training-only cross-validation selected standardised logistic regression over a majority baseline and a restricted random forest. Average precision was 0.983 in cross-validation. The untouched test classified 12 standard and three important products correctly.

What the result means

The 15-product test is small. Perfect performance on this simulation does not establish performance in another store or future period. Higher wallet share and sales, and lower stock cost, were associated with existing importance labels. These are associations, not price elasticity estimates.

Recommendation

Use the model to support category-expert review. Confirm definitions, recover missing records and validate on new stores and later periods before operational use. Review Crunchy Fig Bites and Cocoa Drizzles, whose diagnostic out-of-fold predictions disagree with their standard labels.

Reproduce the analysis

Python analysis and validation checks ↗

Download analyse.py and requirements.txt from that folder. Create a data folder beside the script. Download the raw and complete CSVs from the simulation page and name them data/raw.csv and data/supplied_complete.csv. Run python -m pip install -r requirements.txt, then python analyse.py. The script produces quality summaries, model comparisons and holdout predictions.

Sources and limitations

Oliver Wyman simulation and source downloads ↗. The linked Task 3 brief was inaccessible, so the model follows the available cereal project brief and complete dataset. No store identifier, reporting year or confirmed currency was provided. Raw missing values were preserved rather than fabricated.

Recommendation and success measures
