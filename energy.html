Putting energy trends in context

How has UK final energy consumption changed, and which sectors deserve closer attention?

The finding

Final consumption fell 12.3% from 1970 to 2024, while increasing 2.6% from 2023 to 2024. Transport represents 42.2% of reported final consumption in 2024.

How I approached it

Analysed Table C1 in the 2025 ECUK workbook snapshot across 55 years. Corrected the conversion to 1 ktoe = 0.01163 TWh, preserved missing cells, and checked sector totals without double-counting aggregates.

What I would do next

Prioritize transport for deeper demand analysis, then add activity, weather and efficiency data before explaining changes or setting targets.

Scope and limitations

The verified historical analysis describes a fixed workbook release; it is not a causal study or emissions inventory. Electricity cannot be labelled renewable from this table. The separate backtest and recovered original research figures are identified individually in the gallery below.
