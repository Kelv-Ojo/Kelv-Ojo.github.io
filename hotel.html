Understanding booking cancellations

How do cancellation flag rates vary by hotel, and how can the reporting stay consistent?

Area
Hospitality operations
Tools
SQL · Python · Excel
Scope
Historical / educational analysis

The finding

37.0% of bookings have the source cancellation flag, including no-shows. City Hotel is at 41.7%, compared with 27.8% for Resort Hotel.

How I approached it

Retained all 119,390 source bookings, built SQL reporting tables and a star-schema export, and reconciled joins and monthly totals against pandas. An Excel reporting source covers the full snapshot.

What I would do next

Investigate channel and lead-time mix, then test targeted confirmation or communication trials. Compare matching months because the years have incomplete coverage.

Scope and limitations

This is a historical 2015–2017 snapshot, not current hotel performance. Bookings are not unique guests. The original Power BI artifact has unresolved scope and date-axis issues; the verified metrics here come from SQL and Python.
